Watch at your own pace

Every competition has its own current episode, set by its creator, so groups on different seasons and paces never see each other's results. Scores, standings, and predictions only reflect what your group has actually watched.

How scoring works

Predict the game before it starts

After the draft, every participant answers 11 predictions. Correct calls earn up to 44 bonus points.

Draft night, every night

Share a link and friends join with a free account, then everyone takes turns picking from the full cast in real time on any device. Browsing seasons and castaways never needs an account.

Outwit your friends after the draft

Package active players into an offer, send it to another participant, and negotiate your way back into contention. Deals take effect at the next episode reveal, so past points stay put.
